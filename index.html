What kinds of projects do you take on?+

We work on residential, commercial, and industrial construction, including new construction, renovations, and expansions. Tell us about your project so we can discuss the right approach.

How do I request a quote?+

Use our project inquiry form and share your scope, location, timeline, and budget considerations. Our team will contact you to discuss the details and prepare a proposal.

Is Landmark Construction Group licensed and insured?+

Yes. Landmark Construction Group is licensed and insured to operate in our service area. Contact our team if you need documentation for your project.

Can I ask about a service that is not listed?+

Absolutely. Every project is different. Contact us to discuss the work you need, including specialized or industrial construction requests.

Where is Landmark based?+

Our office is at 48720 Van Dyke Avenue, Shelby Township, MI 48317. We serve construction clients in Michigan. Call us to confirm availability for your project location.
